**No more double voicemails**

### Fixed
- Voicemails no longer show up twice after switching from another phone app (such as the stock GrapheneOS dialer). Once Dialtone has downloaded a message itself, the old app's copy is deleted from the phone, keeping its transcript and whether you'd listened to it. Messages on your carrier's server aren't affected.
- The new-voicemail notification and on-phone transcription count each message once.
- Turning Visual Voicemail off now deletes every voicemail stored on the phone for that SIM, including copies saved by another phone app, not just Dialtone's own. With another SIM still on, only the switched-off SIM's messages are removed.
