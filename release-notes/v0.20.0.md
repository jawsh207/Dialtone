**Home screen widgets and app shortcuts**

### New
- Favorites widget: your favorite contacts as a grid of faces, in the same order as on Home. Tap a face to call. Make it wider for more columns.
- Voicemail widget: how many messages are new, plus the newest caller, time and the start of the transcript. Tap it to open the Voicemail tab.
- Speed dial widget: one contact on your home screen. Dialtone asks who (and which number) when you place it; tap to call. On Android 12 and later, long-press it and choose reconfigure to pick someone else.
- Long-press the Dialtone icon for Keypad, Voicemail, and the three favorites you call most (tap one to call).

### Improved
- Widgets and the favorites in the long-press menu update a few seconds after your contacts, calls or voicemail change, even when Dialtone is closed.
