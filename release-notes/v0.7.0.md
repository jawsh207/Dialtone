**Call log filters and a Keypad tab**

### New
- **Call log filters** on the Home tab, above Favorites: All, Missed, Dialed, Contacts (calls with saved contacts) and Unknown (calls with unsaved numbers).
- **Keypad tab** in place of Contacts. Opening it slides the dial pad up over a **Suggested** list of the people you call most, then your favorites. Typing switches the list to matching contacts. Scroll the list or tap the arrow to tuck the pad away; the dial pad button brings it back.

### Improved
- Tapping a phone number in another app opens the Keypad tab with the number filled in.
- The floating dial pad button is gone, since the Keypad tab replaces it. Contacts are still a search away, and the people you call show up in Suggested.
