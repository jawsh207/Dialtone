**Dialtone 0.1.0 — first release**

### New
- Phone app built with Material 3 Expressive: Home (favorites and recent calls), keypad, Contacts and Voicemail tabs
- In-call screen with mute, keypad, speaker, hold and a SIM picker for dual-SIM phones; full-screen incoming-call alerts
- Built-in visual voicemail (OMTP, CVVM and Verizon VVM3 carriers, including Spectrum Mobile): playback, carrier transcripts, read/delete sync, and new-voicemail notifications
- Voicemail settings with per-SIM on/off, Sync now and an on-device diagnostics log
- App icon: the GrapheneOS dialer handset in white on green

### Improved
- Same look as the Contacts app: wallpaper colours, Google Sans Flex, compact bottom bar with outlined/filled tab icons, standard keypad button
- Optimized (R8) signed builds that install as updates
