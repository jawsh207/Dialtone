**Manage Favorites in Dialtone**

### New
- **Favorites always show on Home**, even when you haven't set any, with a round **Add** button at the end of the row. Tap it to pick a contact from a searchable list and add them to Favorites.
- **Add to Favorites / Remove from Favorites** when you expand a contact in the Contacts list.

### Install note
- The first time you add or remove a favorite, Dialtone asks permission to change contacts. Favorites are your contacts' "starred" flag, so changes also show in your Contacts app.
