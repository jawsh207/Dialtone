**Contacts are back on Home**

### New
- A **Contacts** button on the Home tab, next to Favorites, opens your contacts list right there. The search bar shows a back arrow to return to Home (Back works too).
- **Favorites** can be hidden: tap the chevron next to "Favorites" to collapse or expand the row. Dialtone remembers your choice.

### Improved
- The Keypad tab's dial pad now always stays open, with the Suggested list scrolling behind it. The hide arrow and floating dial pad button are gone.
- Call log filters no longer show a check mark; the highlighted button shows the active filter.
