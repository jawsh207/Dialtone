**On-device voicemail transcription**

### New
- **Transcribe on this phone** (Voicemail settings, off by default): voicemails without a carrier transcript are turned into text right on your phone using the open-source Moonshine speech model. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- The inbox shows "Transcribing…" while a message is being transcribed, and "Transcribed on this phone" under transcripts Dialtone made.

### Improved
- Carrier transcripts always take priority: if your carrier sends one, it's kept and never replaced.
- Turning the option off removes the unpacked speech model from storage; transcripts already made stay.

### Install note
- The app is larger (about 38 MB) because the speech model is bundled. Installs as an update over earlier versions.
