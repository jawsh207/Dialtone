**Send to voicemail**

### New
- **Send to voicemail:** calls from numbers on this list don't ring; they go straight to voicemail. The call still shows in your call log, without a missed-call notification.
  - **Contacts list:** expand a contact and tap **Send to voicemail** (covers all their numbers).
  - **Call log:** long-press a call and tap **Send to voicemail**.
  - **Voicemail settings:** the **Send to voicemail** card lists the numbers, lets you remove them, and adds new ones by typing a number or picking a contact.

### Install note
- Dialtone declines these calls automatically, just as if you tapped Decline, and your carrier then forwards the caller to voicemail. If a listed number still rings, please share the voicemail log (Voicemail settings › Diagnostics).
