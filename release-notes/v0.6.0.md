**Tidier Voicemail tab**

### Improved
- The voicemail settings gear now sits at the right of the search bar while you're on the Voicemail tab.
- "Call voicemail" is now a single button at the end of the list, below your messages (or below "No voicemails"), instead of in the header.
- The "Visual voicemail isn't available" card no longer has a Details button that only opened settings.
- The Transcribe voicemail setting no longer names a specific app: it tries to transcribe with your phone's speech-to-text app, and says that whether it works depends on that app.
- "Change voice input" now opens Language & region settings instead of the default assistant picker.
