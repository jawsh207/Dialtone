**A–Z quick scroll in Contacts**

### New
- **A–Z quick scroll** on the right of the Contacts list, as in the Contacts app. Tap a letter to jump to it, or drag along the strip; the current letter shows large beside it.

### Improved
- When you expand a contact, the call button now comes before the text button.

### Fixed
- In Settings, the back arrow and title were black on the dark theme; they now follow the theme.
