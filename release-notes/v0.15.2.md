**Settings that match the rest of your apps**

### Improved
- **Settings** is now a full page laid out like the Contacts, Zino and Twill apps: a back arrow and title, then sections with coloured headers and rows grouped into rounded cards, with the version at the bottom.
- Sections: **Visual voicemail** (a switch per SIM, and Check for new voicemail), **Transcription**, **Send to voicemail**, **Calls** and **Troubleshooting**.
- The voicemail log is now under **Troubleshooting**, where you can view, copy or clear it. It replaces the Diagnostics button.
