**Reply with a message, and more Settings**

### New
- **Reply with message** on incoming calls: decline and text the caller one of your quick responses, or write your own. The text goes through your default messaging app.
- **Settings › Calls**:
  - **Blocked numbers** opens Android's block list.
  - **Calling accounts** covers your SIMs, call forwarding, call waiting and caller ID.
  - **Quick responses** lets you edit the four texts offered when declining a call.

### Improved
- The **Settings** gear is now in the search bar on every tab, and the Contacts list. The sheet it opens is titled Settings, with Visual voicemail as its first section.
- Android's own "Call settings" and "Quick responses" links now open Dialtone's Settings.
