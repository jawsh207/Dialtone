**Transcription through your speech app, and a much smaller download**

### New
- **Transcribe voicemail** now uses your phone's speech recognizer, the app chosen as voice input (for example Distillery), instead of a speech model built into Dialtone. Voicemails without a carrier transcript are sent to it and the text is saved with the message. Off by default; carrier transcripts are never replaced.
- The settings card shows which recognizer is in use and links to the voice input setting. If it isn't Distillery, it notes that the app may not accept recordings or may send audio off the phone.

### Improved
- The app is about 3 MB again (was about 44 MB), since the speech model is no longer bundled.
- The whole voicemail is transcribed, not just up to the first pause, and in your phone's language.
- Transcription results, including the reason for any refusal, are written to the voicemail log (Voicemail settings › Diagnostics).

### Fixed
- With transcription on, new voicemails could wait to download, and to notify, until Dialtone was opened. Transcription now runs separately, so it can't hold up downloads or notifications.
- A voicemail could be sent to the recognizer twice when it arrived while Dialtone was being opened.

### Install note
- Transcription needs Android 13 or later and a speech recognizer that accepts recordings, such as Distillery. Set it under your phone's voice input settings.
