**Important voicemail fix — please update**

### Fixed
- In v0.2.1, turning Visual Voicemail off could delete your messages from your carrier's voicemail server, not just from the phone. Turning it off now removes only Dialtone's own copies from the phone; your carrier's server is never touched.
- Voicemails another phone app (such as the stock dialer) had stored are no longer deleted when Dialtone hides duplicates. Android can't delete another app's copies without also queuing them for deletion on the carrier, so duplicates are now hidden instead.
- On first launch, Dialtone restores the other phone app's copies of your messages that v0.2.0–v0.2.1 had marked deleted, as long as their audio is still on the phone. Nothing is sent to your carrier.

### Install note
- Don't re-enable the stock dialer before installing this update: it would carry out those pending deletes and remove its copies for good.
